# Allergen & Dietary Protocol

A guest tells you they have a dietary requirement. Treat it as safety-critical.
Never improvise.

## Standard requirements we cover

- **Gluten-free**: pastas, breads, sauces available; dedicated prep area
- **Nut allergy**: ALL kitchen stations briefed; mark guest in PMS
- **Vegan**: separate menu card; tofu/seitan available
- **Vegetarian**: separate menu card; eggs/dairy ok
- **Halal**: certified supplier for meats, lamb is the lead protein
- **Kosher**: pre-arranged only, 24h notice, dedicated supplier
- **Dairy-free**: alternatives in stock (oat / almond milk)
- **Shellfish allergy**: separate prep, station briefed
- **Egg allergy**: bakery briefed, alternatives available

## Workflow (allergen request)

1. **Acknowledge to guest immediately** with specific yes/no on each ask.
2. **Tag guest in PMS** so kitchen sees it on every order.
3. **Brief Exec Chef** if novel (e.g., severe nut allergy + Group).
4. **Confirm at next service** — verify with floor staff and kitchen.

## Common guest queries

- "Do you have gluten-free breakfast?" → "Yes — we have GF pastries, fresh
  fruit, eggs cooked separately, and oats. I'll mark it on your folio."
- "I'm vegan, what can you do for dinner?" → "We have a 4-course vegan
  tasting menu and our chef can adapt any à la carte item — would you like
  me to book a table?"
- "I have a severe nut allergy" → "Got it — I'm tagging it now, and our
  Executive Chef will brief the kitchen for every meal of your stay. We
  also have an EpiPen in the medical kit at reception."

## Banned answers

- "We will try our best" — no. We either can or can't, say which.
- "I'll check with the kitchen" — internally yes, but tell the guest
  directly with the answer.
