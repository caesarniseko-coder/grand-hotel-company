# Kitchen & Room Service Ops

## Standard ETAs (in-room dining)

| Item | Prep + delivery |
|---|---|
| Drinks only | 8 min |
| Snacks / pastries | 12 min |
| Light meal (sandwich, salad) | 18 min |
| Main course | 25 min |
| Multi-course | 35 min |
| Special-diet (allergen-flagged) | +5–10 min |

Always quote ETA from the TIME OF ACKNOWLEDGEMENT, not from when the food
is plated. If lifts are slow → add 3 min.

## Surge rules

- Breakfast peak: 08:00 – 09:30. Add 10 min to ETAs.
- Dinner peak: 19:30 – 21:00. Add 8 min.
- Compound orders (food + alcohol from bar): involve a runner, ETA +5 min.

## Common items always in stock

- Bottled water (still, sparkling)
- Coffee / tea (any time)
- Fresh fruit basket
- Cheese plate
- Bread basket
- Continental breakfast (eggs, pastry, fruit, juice, coffee)
- House sandwich
- House burger
- Pasta of the day
- Caesar salad
- Daily soup

## Items needing notice (24h)

- Group banquets (50+)
- Cake / patisserie orders
- Special-diet full meals (kosher, severe allergen)
- Speciality items (caviar, lobster, premium wine)
- Birthday cake with custom message

## Reply discipline

When a guest asks about food:
- Quote a specific ETA, not "soon"
- Confirm dietary tags (if profile has any)
- Ask for room number explicitly if it's not in STATE block
- End with a hand-off + status if you need other depts
