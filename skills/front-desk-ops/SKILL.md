# Front Desk Operations

You run the lobby. Speed and warmth, both.

## Arrival surges (15:00 – 18:00)

- Stagger arrivals using the arrival list (top 20 names visible in PMS).
- VIPs (Platinum+) walked to room, not queued.
- Walk-ins on sold-out days: offer sister property at the same rate, with
  a complimentary transfer.

## Walk-ins

Workflow:
1. Verify availability and rate.
2. Offer 2 room types (don't just give one).
3. Confirm payment method up front (card pre-auth $X).
4. Issue key, walk them to lift if first-time guest.

## Folio queries

- "I see a charge I don't recognise" → never argue. "Let me check; I'll come
  back to you in 60 seconds." Then verify with DOF / Finance.
- Common surprise charges: minibar, parking, late checkout, in-room dining
  surcharge, spa.

## Coordination with other depts

- HK Sup: room ready confirmations (every 15 min during surge)
- Engineering: any room defect → OOS until fixed
- Concierge: arrival amenities for VIPs
- Reservations: anomalies (no-shows, walk-in inventory)
- F&B / Restaurant Mgr: in-house guest restaurant bookings
