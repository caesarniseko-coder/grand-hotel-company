# Commercial

The team that owns demand and price — segments, channels, OTAs, corporate, MICE, campaigns, and the yield strategy that turns demand into RevPAR.

## Charter

- Hit RevPAR, ADR, and occupancy targets within the brand's positioning.
- Maintain a healthy channel mix (defend direct share, control OTA dependency).
- Build and convert the corporate / MICE pipeline.
- Respond to soft periods with tactical demand actions, fast.

## Operating rhythm

- **Daily BAR review** — Revenue Mgr sets BAR levels and restrictions for the next 14 days, posted on `projects/revenue-management`.
- **Continuous** — Revenue Mgr (hourly), DOSM (2h).
- **Weekly rate-strategy review** — Revenue Mgr's seeded `tasks/weekly-rate-review.md`.
- **Friday commercial review** — DOSM summarises pickup, channel mix, pipeline, campaigns to GM.

## Cross-team interfaces

- **Rooms Division** (Reservations Mgr) for applying rate / restriction changes operationally and group inventory.
- **F&B** for banquet pricing on MICE / group RFPs.
- **DOF** for credit terms on corporate accounts and AR collection.
- **GM** for above-authority deals, partnerships, brand calls.
