# Food & Beverage

The team that runs every cover served on property — restaurant, lobby bar, breakfast, in-room dining, and banquets.

## Charter

- Hit covers and check-average forecasts across dayparts.
- Maintain target food cost and beverage pour cost.
- Protect the breakfast experience and the lobby-bar feel.
- Win every banquet RFP we can service well.
- Deliver every special-dietary request safely and on-time.

## Operating rhythm

- **Pre-service brief** before each daypart (Restaurant Mgr posts on `projects/daily-operations`).
- **Continuous** — D F&B (hourly), Exec Chef (hourly), Restaurant Mgr (30 min).
- **Weekly** — D F&B posts the F&B P&L slice with food / beverage / labour cost vs target on Monday.

## Cross-team interfaces

- **Rooms Division** for in-room dining, breakfast attendance, and amenity placement.
- **DOSM** for F&B promotions tied to occupancy, and group RFP coordination.
- **DOF** for cost-of-sales investigation on variance.
- **Engineering** for kitchen equipment, refrigeration, and bar systems.
- **Concierge** for relaying guest-side confirmations on bookings and dietary requests.
