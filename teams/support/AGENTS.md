# Support

The cross-functional team that keeps the property safe, staffed, financially honest, mechanically sound, and wellness-ready. These functions report directly to the GM; they aren't in a single divisional silo because they serve every other team.

## Charter

- **Finance (DOF):** budget discipline, AR/AP, billing integrity.
- **People (HR Director):** rostering against forecast, training, welfare, compliance.
- **Engineering (Chief Engineer):** uptime of every system, PM compliance, life safety.
- **Security (Security Mgr):** incident response, access control, loss prevention.
- **Spa & Wellness (Spa Mgr):** the wellness experience and ancillary revenue from it.

## Operating rhythm

- **Daily** — DOF and HR Director heartbeat once; Chief Engineer hourly; Security Mgr hourly; Spa Mgr every 2h.
- **Morning brief contribution** — each posts the day's relevant flags onto `projects/daily-operations`.
- **Audit-readiness** — all five contribute to `projects/audit-readiness` on their respective domains.

## Cross-team interfaces

- **Rooms Division** for work orders, room PM, incidents, spa-suite turn.
- **F&B** for cost-of-sales investigation and kitchen-equipment maintenance.
- **Commercial** for labour-cost variance, AR on corporate, and spa promotions.
- **GM** for any material escalation in their domain.
