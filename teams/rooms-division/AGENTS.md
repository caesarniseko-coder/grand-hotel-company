# Rooms Division

The team that owns the guest's room journey — pre-arrival, check-in, in-stay, departure, and the room itself.

## Charter

- Hit the daily occupancy and ADR forecast set with Revenue Mgr.
- Deliver every room ready-on-time, defect-free, and presentation-perfect.
- Resolve in-room and front-of-house issues fast, with a recovery touch.
- Maintain audit-grade room quality and PM compliance jointly with Engineering.

## Operating rhythm

- **Morning standup (08:30)** — DOR posts the day's brief on `projects/daily-operations`: arrivals, departures, OOO/OOS, VIPs, group movements, sold-out / soft-period rate posture.
- **Continuous** — Front Office Mgr (15 min), Concierge (15 min), Housekeeping Sup (30 min), Executive Housekeeper (hourly), Reservations Mgr (30 min), DOR (hourly) heartbeat their respective vitals.
- **EOD** — DOR posts the day's wrap (final occupancy, ADR, ALOS, pickup, top complaints).

## Cross-team interfaces

- **D F&B** for in-room dining, breakfast operation, and amenity coordination.
- **Engineering** for room-defect work orders and PM rotation.
- **DOSM / Revenue Mgr** for inventory restrictions, rate moves, and group pickup.
- **Security Mgr** for incidents involving guests or rooms.
- **GM** for VIP / escalation / strategic calls.
