# Weekly Rate Review

Each Monday at 10:00, Revenue Mgr posts the week-ahead rate strategy on `projects/revenue-management`. DOSM reviews and signs off; GM is informed.

## What to include

- **Pickup last week** — actuals by segment, channel, and rate plan vs forecast.
- **Pace vs LY** — for the next 30 / 60 / 90 days, broken by occupancy vs ADR contribution.
- **Comp-set position** — last week's average rate index, where we sit by daypart.
- **BAR ladder for next 14 days** — proposed levels with reasoning, restrictions in force.
- **Soft-period flags** — dates in the next 60 days under-pacing with proposed action.
- **Strong-demand dates** — dates worth restricting (MLOS / CTA) or raising rate.
- **Channel mix** — direct share trend, OTA-dependency flag.
- **Campaign performance** — last week's campaigns: pickup vs expected, ROI.

## Handoffs

- **DOSM** signs off on tactical responses to soft periods and approves campaign briefs.
- **GM** is briefed on any material rate move or forecast revision.
- **Reservations Mgr** applies the approved BAR ladder and restrictions.
