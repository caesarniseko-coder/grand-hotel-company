# Morning Briefing

Seed today's `daily-operations` thread with the day's brief.

## What to include

- **Last night's wrap** — final occupancy, ADR, ALOS, top complaints from yesterday (one-line each).
- **Today's posture** — occupancy on the books, sold-out status, soft-period flag.
- **Arrivals** — total, VIPs by name, groups, large parties needing coordination.
- **Departures** — total, late check-outs requested, any disputes flagged at folio.
- **Rooms readiness** — current OOO / OOS count, any blocking issues, deep-clean rotation today.
- **F&B day** — covers forecast for breakfast / lunch / dinner, banquets scheduled, any 86'd items expected.
- **Commercial flags** — rate posture for the next 14 days, comp-set move worth noting, RFPs needing response.
- **Engineering** — top open WOs by impact, any PM that touches occupied rooms today.
- **People** — staffing gaps, training scheduled, welfare flag.
- **Today's priorities (3 max)** — what the property needs to nail today.

## Handoffs

Once posted, every HOD reads it on their next heartbeat and pulses their domain in reply.
