# Housekeeping Board

Keep the live rooms board honest — Front Office needs vacant-ready counts they can trust.

## What to update each cycle

- **Departures** — how many cleaned, how many inspected, how many still in progress.
- **Stayovers** — in-progress / done.
- **Vacant-ready** — final count that Front Office can hand to walk-ins / early arrivals.
- **OOO / OOS** — with reason (defect / deep clean / hold) and ETA back in service.
- **Priority turns** — any room being expedited (early arrival / VIP / room change) with assigned attendant and target time.
- **Defects flagged** — list any new work-order asks raised with Chief Engineer.

## Handoffs

- **Front Office Mgr** consumes vacant-ready counts and priority-turn progress.
- **Executive Housekeeper** consumes plan adherence and surfaces gaps to DOR.
- **Chief Engineer** receives any new work orders linked from here.
