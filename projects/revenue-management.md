# Revenue Management

The running thread for pricing and demand. Revenue Mgr posts hourly; DOSM posts the demand-side actions.

## What lives here

- **Daily BAR ladder** — BAR levels for the next 14 days, with reasoning.
- **Restrictions in force** — MLOS, CTA, closed-to-arrival, with start/end dates.
- **Forecast** — rolling 30/60/90/365 day occupancy / ADR / RevPAR. Updated when material.
- **Pickup pulse** — last 24h pickup by segment and channel.
- **Comp-set read** — comp-set rate over the next 14 days, position decision.
- **Campaigns** — DOSM's tactical campaigns with expected pickup and actual results.
- **RFP pipeline** — corporate / MICE inquiries and their status.

## Cadence

- Hourly pricing-action pulse from Revenue Mgr.
- 2-hourly demand pulse from DOSM.
- **Weekly rate-strategy review** every Monday (see `tasks/weekly-rate-review.md`).
- **Friday commercial review** — DOSM summarises pickup, channel mix, pipeline, campaigns to GM.

## Cross-team

- **Reservations Mgr** to apply restrictions / rate moves operationally.
- **D F&B** for banquet pricing on group RFPs.
- **DOF** for credit terms on corporate accounts.
- **GM** for above-authority deals.
