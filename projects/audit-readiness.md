# Audit Readiness

A standing thread that keeps the property audit-grade every day, so a brand-standard / quality / life-safety audit can happen any morning and pass.

## What lives here

- **Brand-standard checklist status** — by category (rooms, public areas, F&B, spa, service standards, signage, uniforms). Updated weekly by the relevant HOD.
- **Life-safety compliance** — fire / sprinkler / lift / evac drill logs current (Chief Engineer + Security Mgr).
- **PM compliance** — room rotation, HVAC, lift, kitchen equipment cycles on schedule (Chief Engineer).
- **Training compliance** — brand-standard, safety, service-recovery training current (HR Director).
- **Public-area inspections** — daily lobby / restaurant / spa / gym / executive lounge inspection notes (Executive Housekeeper).
- **Audit findings & corrective actions** — when a finding lands, opened as its own issue and tracked to closure here.

## Cadence

- Daily public-area inspection (Executive Housekeeper).
- Weekly brand-standard category review (rotating HODs).
- Monthly life-safety / PM compliance summary (Chief Engineer + Security Mgr).
- Quarterly mock-audit (GM convenes).

## Cross-team

- Every HOD contributes their domain. The GM owns the standing posture and signs off on corrective actions.
