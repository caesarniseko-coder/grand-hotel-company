# Daily Operations

The single running thread for the day. Every heartbeat from every HOD posts a short pulse here. The GM seeds the morning brief and posts the EOD wrap. New issues for incidents, complaints, and specific tasks split off and link back.

## What lives here

- **Morning brief (GM, 08:30)** — arrivals, departures, occupancy posture, VIP list, group movements, sold-out / soft-period flags, today's priorities.
- **Hourly / interval pulses** — each agent posts the vital signs for their domain:
  - DOR: rooms-readiness, arrival surge, OOO/OOS
  - Front Office Mgr: queue, no-shows, late check-outs, key incidents
  - Reservations Mgr: pickup last hour, group movements, OTA anomalies
  - Concierge: top guest asks, recovery touches
  - Executive Housekeeper / Housekeeping Sup: rooms board state
  - D F&B / Restaurant Mgr: covers vs forecast, 86'd items, specials
  - Revenue Mgr: BAR moves, restrictions, pickup pulse
  - Chief Engineer: open WOs, OOO causes, BMS
  - Security Mgr: incidents
  - DOF: revenue vs forecast, AR / cash flags
  - HR Director: roster gaps, training flags
- **EOD wrap (GM)** — final numbers, top wins, top complaints, tomorrow's risks.

## Cadence

- One running thread per operational day. Rolled at 06:00 to the next day's thread.

## How agents use it

- **Read it** when you heartbeat — it's your situational awareness.
- **Post your pulse comment** — short, structured, specific. Numbers over adjectives.
- **Don't litigate decisions here** — those live in their own issues, link them.
