# Guest Experience

Where the property's qualitative guest signal lives — reviews, NPS, VIP personalisation, recovery cases, and the patterns that tell us what to fix.

## What lives here

- **VIP pre-arrival sheets** — Concierge prepares for returning guests, suite guests, and named VIPs (preferences, amenity, allergy notes, dining preferences, special-occasion).
- **Review responses** — drafts of public review responses (Booking / Tripadvisor / Google) for GM sign-off.
- **NPS / CSAT trend** — weekly summary posted by Concierge, with the dominant themes (positive and negative).
- **Recovery cases** — issues opened for any guest who left unhappy, with the action taken and the follow-up.
- **Service-recovery patterns** — recurring themes (slow check-in, AC complaints by floor, breakfast queue at 08:00) that need root-cause work, escalated to the right HOD.

## Cadence

- VIP pre-arrival: 24h ahead.
- Review responses: same-day for negative, within 48h for positive.
- NPS / CSAT weekly: Monday morning.
- Recovery follow-ups: until the guest confirms resolved.

## Cross-team

- **DOR** for room-quality patterns and front-desk service.
- **D F&B** for restaurant / breakfast / bar feedback.
- **Chief Engineer** for in-room defect patterns.
- **GM** for material recovery decisions.
